1. Publish a new model

Export your SolidWorks part or assembly to GLB before uploading. Native .sldprt and .sldasm files are not supported directly.

Blender export tips (NFR2 — keep it under 5s load)

File → Export → glTF 2.0 (.glb) → enable Draco compression in the export panel. Keep textures ≤ 2K and triangle count reasonable for mobile GPUs.
